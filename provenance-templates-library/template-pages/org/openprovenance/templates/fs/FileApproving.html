• Name: FileApproving
  • Fully Qualified Name: org.openprovenance.templates.fs.FileApproving
  • IRI: https://openprovenance.org/templates/org/openprovenance/templates/fs/FileApproving
  • Purpose: The template FileApproving describes the approval of a machine-learning pipeline by a manager, on the basis of its validation score.
  • Context: The template is useful for describing the sign-off step of a machine-learning workflow, in which a trained pipeline is released for use once its performance is deemed acceptable.
  • Design considerations: The ability to identify the pipeline (before and after approval) and to keep a signed record of the approval.
  • Automation: ttfs/config-fs.json

  • Details:

    At the start, there is a pipeline, for instance produced by FileTraining, and a score, for instance produced by FileValidating. After the approving activity, there are two outputs: the approved pipeline approved_pipeline, and an approval record approval_record, which carries a signature and refers to the approved pipeline. An agent manager controls the approving activity and follows an approval method approval_method; both outputs are attributed to the manager.

    The template is an instantiation of Product2-2: each output is derived from both inputs, so the approved pipeline and the approval record both depend on the pipeline and on its score.

    Some predefined, self-explanatory attributes have been adopted, such as filename and path for the approved pipeline.

    An example is a manager who reviews the accuracy score of a newly trained classifier and signs off its deployment.
