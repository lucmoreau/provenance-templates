• Name: FileTraining
  • Fully Qualified Name: org.openprovenance.templates.fs.FileTraining
  • IRI: https://openprovenance.org/templates/org/openprovenance/templates/fs/FileTraining
  • Purpose: The template FileTraining describes the training of a machine-learning pipeline on a training dataset.
  • Context: The template is useful for describing the model-fitting step of a machine-learning workflow.
  • Design considerations: The ability to identify the trained pipeline and the dataset it was trained on.
  • Automation: ttfs/config-fs.json

  • Details:

    At the start, there is a dataset training_dataset; after the training activity, there is a pipeline. An agent engineer controls the training activity and uses a method method (such as a script or programme); the pipeline is attributed to the engineer.

    The template is an instantiation of Transforming1, with training_dataset as its input and pipeline as its output.

    Some predefined, self-explanatory attributes have been adopted, such as filename and path.

    The pipeline can then be assessed by FileValidating and approved by FileApproving.
