PROV-TEMPLATE

The provenance template family of documents · Unofficial Drafts, 16 October 2026 · Editor: Luc Moreau

A provenance template is a PROV document whose identifiers, attributes and values may be variables. A set of bindings gives values to the variables, and instantiation replaces them to produce an ordinary PROV document. The family consists of five documents.

  • PROV-TEMPLATE-OVERVIEWnon-normative

    An overview of provenance templates: the approach, its artefacts, and a guide to the family.

  • PROV-TEMPLATE-PRIMERnon-normative

    A step-by-step introduction through the provenance of a nightly file processing pipeline.

  • PROV-TEMPLATEnormative

    Templates, variables and the tmpl: vocabulary; bindings; bindings schemas; template indexes; template transformation files. Non-normative: the command line, executables, Maven plugin and template compiler.

  • PROV-TEMPLATE-ALGORITHMSnormative

    The instantiation and merge algorithms, and their error situations.

  • PROV-TEMPLATE-LIBRARYnon-normative

    The library of templates: identification, organisation, derivation, and its families of templates.
