OpenProvenance vocabulary

Namespace: https://openprovenance.org/ns/openprov#   Prefix: openprov

The vocabulary the OpenProvenance template library and ProvToolbox use in provenance documents beyond PROV-DM and provext: the statements a summarising relation was folded from, the roles of the parties to a change of collection, and the kinds of activity and derivation that change a collection. Distinct from provext, the minimal extension PROV-JSONLD needs for its regular JSON shape, whose classes this vocabulary builds on.

Imports https://openprovenance.org/ns/provext#.

Object Properties

Each property is declared as owl:ObjectProperty; it is optional on the relation named by its domain and points at a statement that relation summarises.

On prov:Attribution and provext:Membership

openprov:hadActivityhttps://openprovenance.org/ns/openprov#hadActivity
rdfs:labelhad activity
rdfs:commentThe activity the relation arose from: for an attribution, the activity that generated, invalidated or derived the entity while the agent was associated with it; for a membership, the activity that placed the member in the collection. A sub-property of prov:hadActivity, whose domain PROV-O states as prov:Influence and, more precisely, as the union of Delegation, Derivation, End and Start; an attribution or membership carrying this property is therefore entailed to be an Influence, and, under the precise domain, a member of that union, which PROV-O declares nothing to contradict.
rdfs:subPropertyOfprov:hadActivity
rdfs:domainprov:Attribution ∪ provext:Membership
rdfs:rangeprov:Activity

On prov:Attribution and provext:Membership and prov:Communication and prov:Start and prov:End

openprov:hadGenerationhttps://openprovenance.org/ns/openprov#hadGeneration
rdfs:labelhad generation
rdfs:commentFor an attribution, the generation of the attributed entity by the activity it arose from; for a membership, the generation of the collection by that activity; for a communication, the generation, by the informant, of the entity the informed activity used; for a start or an end, the generation of the trigger entity by the starter or ender activity.
rdfs:domainprov:Attribution ∪ provext:Membership ∪ prov:Communication ∪ prov:Start ∪ prov:End
rdfs:rangeprov:Generation
